Settings

Research engine

Questions run through your own subscription. Both engines share one memory and one history; each answer records which engine produced it. The reviewer checks an answer in a fresh session of the engine that wrote it, which can open the sources it read. ChatGPT (Codex) is the default: in October 2026 Claude Code’s web fetch could not open about two in five of the sources researchers cited (publisher pages and many PDFs), so its answers find fewer readable sources.

Checking…

Sign-in happens on the provider’s own website; no password is entered here.

Adding findings

After an answer is delivered, the reviewer that checks its figure readings, conversions and combined values also checks a sample of the ones the research agent added, to show how often its judgement holds up. The answer never waits for it.

0 (the default) turns it off. Above 0, each answer's open questions join the research queue and the hourly loop asks up to this many a week, using a signed-in engine's spare quota.

Privacy

Your question is sent to the selected engine’s provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) to run the research. The answer, its findings and its activity record stay on this computer. Each finding is labelled with who vouched for it: the research agent, or a separate reviewer that checked it against its source. Your questions and activity records are never included in public releases.
