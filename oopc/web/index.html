Scope

The research agent answers any question connected to this, however marginally, and answers when unsure. Only a question with no connection to it is not researched: its page links to another assistant instead.

A question outside the field but connected to it is answered either way. By default its findings are not saved, so the knowledge base stays on the field.

Adding findings

The reviewer also checks a sample of added findings, to show how often the agent’s judgement holds up. Answers never wait for it.

The reviewer compares each new finding with the saved findings most related to it: the same polymer and property, and other readings of its source. An answer with nothing else to review is reviewed only when its values disagree with saved ones.

0 turns it off. Above 0, open questions from your answers join the research queue and the hourly loop asks up to this many a week with spare quota.

Questions asked while others run start at once instead of waiting. Each engine then runs that many sessions together, sharing your subscription’s limits; a follow-up still waits for the answer before it.

Privacy

Your question goes to the selected engine’s provider (OpenAI or Anthropic). The answer, its findings and its activity record stay on this computer, and your questions and activity are never included in public releases.
